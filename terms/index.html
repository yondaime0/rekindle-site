2. The app

Rekindle is a habit tracker. It works offline and without an account. Check-ins, momentum, Comeback and reminders are free and stay free. The free plan includes up to 4 active habits and 30 days of history in the calendar.

3. Rekindle Pro

Rekindle Pro is an optional subscription. It includes unlimited habits, your full history in the calendar and Progress, and cloud sync across your phones.

Prices are shown in the app in your currency before you buy. In the United States they are $0.99 a month or $4.99 a year. Google Play bills the subscription to your Google account; we never see your payment details.

  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
  • If a price changes, Google Play tells you in advance and asks for your consent where the law or Play's rules require it.

6. Cloud sync

Sync is optional and part of Rekindle Pro. It needs Google sign-in and an active Pro subscription. We run it with care, but we offer it on a best-effort basis, without a guarantee that it is always available.

When Pro ends, sync stops. Your phone keeps all your habits and history. Your account keeps its copy for 180 days, then that copy is deleted. If you subscribe again within those 180 days, sync picks up where it left off. You can delete your account and its copy at any time.

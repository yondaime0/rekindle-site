Rekindle

A calm habit tracker for real life. Each habit has momentum, a number from 0 to 100 that follows your recent days. Missing a day lowers it a little. It never resets to zero.

No account needed. Your habits stay on your phone unless you turn on sync with Google in Settings. Sync is part of Rekindle Pro, an optional subscription that also adds unlimited habits and your full history. Check-ins, momentum, Comeback and reminders are always free.

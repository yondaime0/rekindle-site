Delete your Rekindle account

Rekindle works without an account. If you turned on sync with Google, you can delete that account and everything stored with it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Rekindle and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account & sync, tap Delete account.
  3. Choose whether to also remove your habits from this phone, then tap Delete account and Delete for good.

Your phone needs a connection for this. Your account, your synced habits, check-ins and comebacks are deleted from our database right away.

By email

If you no longer have the app, write to yondaime869@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Rekindle account, from the Google address you signed in with. We delete the account and all its data within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email, name, profile picture link, Google account ID and account ID, and its sign-in sessions.
  • All synced habits, schedules, check-ins, comebacks and the day-start setting.

Nothing is kept. The only copies left are in the hosting provider's daily backups, where the plan includes them, and those are deleted within 7 days. Habits stored only on your phone stay there unless you choose to remove them, and you can keep using Rekindle without an account.
